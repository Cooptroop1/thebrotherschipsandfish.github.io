Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Who we are

The Brothers Chips and Fish runs thebrotherschipsandfish.co.uk. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site, order food, or play the fry game. Contact us at info@thebrotherschipsandfish.co.uk.

2. What we collect

  • Account: Your name, phone number, email and password, so you can log in and order.
  • Pickup orders: The food, the time, your name and phone number.
  • Delivery orders: The same, plus the house, street and postcode. We keep that address on your account so it can fill in next time.
  • Card payments: Pay now is taken by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. Stripe does, under its own privacy policy.
  • Pay later: If we have given you a code, the order is saved against your account and paid when you collect or when the driver arrives. No card payment is taken online.
  • Fry game: A game name, your email and a password, plus your score. The leaderboard shows the game name and the score, not your email.
  • Cookies and analytics: Google Analytics tells us which pages are used, on what device, and for how long. We use that to improve the site.

We do not run adverts on this site, and we do not use Google AdSense.

3. How we use it

  • To take the order, cook it, and hand it over or deliver it.
  • To check a delivery postcode is inside the area for that day. The postcode is sent to postcodes.io to get a map point. We only keep the address you typed.
  • To show your game name and score on the leaderboard.
  • To email you if we run a game competition. We will not put your email on the board.
  • To see how the site is used, through Google Analytics.

We do not sell your details. Staff can see orders so they can cook and deliver them. We share data with the services that run the site, listed below, and if the law says we must.

4. Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores the accounts, orders and game scores.
  • Stripe takes card payments.
  • Vercel runs the payment check.
  • postcodes.io turns a postcode into a map point for the delivery area.
  • Google Analytics measures visits.
  • OpenStreetMap shows the map. If you tap Directions, Google Maps opens on your phone.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

5. Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies. The login also keeps you signed in. You can block cookies in your browser. Blocking them can stop login and analytics.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your account, orders and game score. Email info@thebrotherschipsandfish.co.uk. We keep orders so we can run the van and look up a cancelled order if you ask what it was. If you want it deleted for good, ask us and we will remove it unless we still need it for tax or a legal claim.

7. Security

The site uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored by the login system. We cannot read them. No internet transmission is completely secure.

8. Other sites

Links to Facebook and Instagram use their own privacy policies once you leave our site.

9. Changes

We will update this page if the site starts collecting something new. The date at the top is the last change.
