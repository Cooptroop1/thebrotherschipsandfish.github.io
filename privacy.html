Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 March 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Brothers Chips and Fish. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website (thebrotherschipsandfish.co.uk) or contact us.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact form: Name, email, message (to respond to enquiries).
  • Cookies & Analytics: Google Analytics tracks visits (pages viewed, time on site, device) to improve our site. We use cookies for basic functionality and analytics.
  • No payment data: We do not process payments online — no card details collected.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to your enquiries via email/form.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to track site usage (via Google Analytics). You can manage cookies via the consent banner or browser settings. Our Cookie Consent tool complies with UK GDPR/ePrivacy rules.

4.1 Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies and similar technologies to serve personalised ads based on your browsing history and interests.

You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and manage or opt out of personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.

5. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict your data. Contact us at info@thebrotherschipsandfish.co.uk to exercise these rights.

6. Security

We use HTTPS and secure hosting. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
