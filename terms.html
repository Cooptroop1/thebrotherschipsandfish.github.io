Order terms

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Who you are ordering from

Online orders are taken by The Brothers Chips and Fish Ltd, company number 14121248. Registered office: 65 Dereham Road, Scarning, Dereham, NR19 2PT. Email: info@thebrotherschipsandfish.co.uk.

2. What you are ordering

An order is for collection at the place and time shown when you book. The price is the price on the site at the time you pay. The minimum order is £15. The maximum order is £100 of food.

Delivery is only available on a day we have switched it on. If it is on, the site shows a £5 delivery charge and you can check the postcode. If it is off, the order is collection only.

3. Paying by card

Card payments are taken by Stripe. We do not see or store your card number. Paying holds that collection time for 5 minutes while you finish. If you do not finish, the time goes back on sale and no money is taken.

A pay later code is only given out on the van. If you use one, no card payment is taken online. You pay when you collect.

4. Cancellations and refunds

The food is cooked for your collection time. It is hot food that will not keep, so the usual 14-day cancellation right does not apply once the order is paid.

If we cancel the order, or it is not what you ordered, we will refund the card payment. If you do not collect it, or you are late and it has been cooked, we do not refund it.

If we are very busy, the food can be a little late. A can might be out of stock, and you may have to choose another one when you collect.

5. Allergies

The food is cooked in one van. Tell us about an allergy before you order. If you are not sure, ask us on the van and do not order that item online.

6. Your account

You need an account to order. Keep your login to yourself. What we do with your name, phone, email and order is in the privacy policy.
